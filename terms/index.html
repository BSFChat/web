5. What you may not do

  • Harass, threaten, stalk, impersonate, or incite violence against anyone.
  • Post content that is intended to humiliate or degrade a specific person, or that sexualises a minor in any way.
  • Send unsolicited bulk messages, run scams, phish for credentials, or distribute malware.
  • Attack the service: probing for vulnerabilities is fine and welcome if you tell us (security@bsfchat.com); denial-of-service, credential stuffing and mass scraping are not.
  • Use someone else's account, or evade a block, ban or account termination.
  • Infringe someone else's copyright or trade marks with content you upload.

There is no tolerance for abusive content or abusive users. Every BSFChat client lets you block a user and report a message; reports reach the administrators of the server the message was sent on. On our servers, we act on reports ourselves. We may remove content, suspend an account or terminate it — with notice where that is reasonable and without it where it is not.

Child sexual abuse material. This is the one point in these terms that is addressed to server operators as well as to users. If you run a BSFChat server, material of this kind must be reported to the authorities in your country, and we expect anyone running a server to do exactly that. On a server we operate, where we are made aware of such material we will report it to the appropriate authorities and terminate the account immediately and permanently. A report keeps its own copy of the reported message even after the message itself is deleted — the privacy policy explains why — and that copy is what makes reporting to an authority possible at all.

Being exact about the limits of that, because the opposite impression would be worse than useless: nothing is scanned. There is no automated detection anywhere in BSFChat — no image scanning, no matching against any database of known material, no classifier — on our servers or in the app, and nobody is reading your messages looking for anything. What we can do is therefore entirely reactive. We act on what is reported to us, and we cannot act on what nobody reports, which is the plainest reason there is to use the report button.
